Before the cases

The BTA project established the starting point

The origin story matters because it explains a decision that would otherwise look like a technology preference.

  1. The work

    A multinational common-protocol collaboration

    The BTA project studied utilization patterns and effectiveness of bone-targeting agents in breast, prostate, and lung cancer with bone metastasis. A standardized protocol was delivered to each data partner.

  2. The cost

    Alignment consumed the effort

    Substantial work was required to align protocol details, ensure appropriate variable definitions at every site, and independently develop analytic programs at each location.

  3. The decision

    Move the standardization upstream

    The next programme stages moved from protocol-only alignment toward common data models and shared analytic programs, while retaining local implementation and review.

What these cases are not

Boundaries that apply to every case on this site

Not a database ranking

Differences between environments reflect population, coding, coverage, and clinical practice. No case ranks a partner against another.

Not a completeness claim

Cohort counts, publication status, and conference acceptance are not published here. They require separate owner and disclosure review.

Not a substitute for feasibility work

A result reproduced in these settings does not establish that a new question is answerable in them. That still requires fit-for-purpose review.
