About IMPRESIVE

Preparedness is the platform

IMPRESIVE is research infrastructure for preparing international multi-database studies across data, methods, collaboration, and interpretation.

The formal name

A complete pathway from databases to decisions

International Multi-database study PREparedness: Databases Standardization, Integration and Visualization for timely Evaluation on Disease and Treatment.

The programme prepares local data environments for common study questions, transparent implementation, quality review, distributed analysis, and contextual interpretation. It does not require person-level partner data to be centralized.

Core message

Prepared databases. Integrated evidence. Timely decisions.

Preparation makes assumptions, data constraints, methods, local responsibilities, and interpretation boundaries explicit before evidence is combined.

What the infrastructure prepares

Four forms of readiness, one study-specific decision

A converted database is not automatically ready for every question. Readiness must be assessed for the proposed study and revisited when the protocol, data period, definitions, or operating environment changes.

Data

Can the environment identify the required population, exposure, outcomes, covariates, time zero, and follow-up?

Method

Can common definitions, quality checks, and analytic programs be implemented consistently?

Collaboration

Can local teams and governance processes support distributed execution and permitted output sharing?

Interpretation

Can differences be understood in the context of coding, populations, coverage, and healthcare practice?

Four pathways of value

IMPRESIVE Impact

Four connected forms of impact show how harmonised data, reusable methods, and multinational collaboration can strengthen evidence generation and participation.

01 / 04

Scientific and academic impact

Harmonisation · Reproducibility · Comparability

We align data and methods to produce transparent, reproducible, and comparable multinational evidence.

IMPRESIVE uses harmonised definitions, a common data model, shared protocols, and common analytical programmes across participating countries. This reduces avoidable differences in study implementation, improves transparency and reproducibility, and enables meaningful comparison of results across populations and healthcare systems.

Clinical, policy, and societal impact

Timeliness · Transportability · Evidence equity

We generate timely and privacy-preserving evidence for clinical, regulatory, and policy decisions.

IMPRESIVE combines multinational data with clinical expertise to accelerate evidence generation for drug safety and healthcare decisions. Its distributed approach keeps patient-level data within each country, while cross-country analyses assess whether findings are transportable across healthcare settings and improve evidence representation for Asian and other underrepresented populations.

Economic, industry, and technological innovation impact

Cost reduction · Reuse · Innovation readiness

We reuse data and analytical infrastructure to reduce research costs and support scalable innovation.

IMPRESIVE reuses validated data structures, cohorts, quality checks, and analytical programmes across studies and countries. Although initial infrastructure requires investment, reuse reduces duplicated work, shortens study timelines, and lowers the cost of subsequent research. The structured environment can also support trial feasibility assessment, external model validation, and future data-driven innovation.

Education and global development impact

Capacity building · Participation · Sustainability

We build shared skills and standards so more countries can participate in multinational research.

IMPRESIVE converts its experience in data mapping, validation, and analysis into shared guidance and training for researchers, clinicians, data personnel, and regulators. This creates a common research language, lowers barriers for emerging partners, and enables participating countries to contribute not only data but also to study design, analysis, and interpretation.

Governance & transparency

Trust is designed into the collaboration

These public principles summarize how IMPRESIVE aims to keep multinational work accountable. Study-specific committees, agreements, approvals, and release procedures must be confirmed before each project begins.

01

Proposal & scope review

Questions, intended uses, roles, feasibility, and priorities are reviewed before execution.

02

Local data control

Person-level data remain within approved local environments; only permitted outputs are shared.

03

Ethics & local approval

Each partner follows the applicable ethics, data-owner, privacy, and institutional requirements.

04

Authorship & publication

Credit, review responsibilities, and dissemination expectations are agreed before public release.

05

Conflict-of-interest disclosure

Relevant interests are declared and managed within the approved collaboration process.

06

Reproducible release

Versioned code, analysis records, quality diagnostics, and interpretation notes support transparent reporting.
