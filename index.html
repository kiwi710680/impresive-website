Leveraging Local Healthcare Data for Global Real-World Evidence

What is IMPRESIVE?

IMPRESIVE stands for International Multi-database study PREparedness: Databases Standardization, Integration and Visualization for timely Evaluation on Disease and Treatment.
IMPRESIVE serves as a research infrastructure designed to facilitate international multi-database studies across four critical domains: data, methods, collaboration, and interpretation.

The IMPRESIVE study workflow

Walk through the architecture

  1. Research question

    Translating clinical, policy, or regulatory questions into robust data analysis approaches. Why preparedness starts here →

  2. Feasibility & fit for purpose

    Evaluating whether local data environments can support the intended analytical strategy. Criteria →

  3. Protocol & variable definition

    Harmonizing definitions across data centers, including analysis plans, code lists, variable specifications, and output formats.Code lists →

  4. Standardization

    Applying Common Data Model structures, such as OMOP or Sentinel-like frameworks, to establish a unified analytical interface tailored to local settings. Routes and mappings →

  5. Quality validation

    Verifying structural conformance, completeness, plausibility, and study-specific validity of converted datasets. Quality framework →

  6. Distributed execution

    Executing standardized analytical programs locally within each data center while documenting site-specific adaptations, exceptions, and limitations. What travels →

  7. Evidence integration

    Aggregating site-level outputs while preserving site-level context. Case portfolio →

  8. Visualization & interpretation

    Presenting country-specific and integrated findings through dynamic visual analytics, clearly highlighting uncertainty and contextual insights. Visualization platform →
