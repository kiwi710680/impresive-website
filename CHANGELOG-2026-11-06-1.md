## Approach

The request was to reduce the impression of too much text through structure rather than rewriting. So this round **keeps the wording** and changes how it is presented: long paragraphs become grids, numbered lists and key lists; a long reference table moves into a disclosure.

Where a sentence had to be split into list items, every item comes from the original sentence. Four places needed new wording; they are listed under "Wording that changed".

## Changes by page

### Home (`index.html`)
1. **What is IMPRESIVE?** The two paragraphs become two columns. Left: the acronym with its initials highlighted (as on About). Right: the four domains (Data, Methods, Collaboration, Interpretation) as linked tiles to Partner, Methods, Join and Accomplishment.
2. **Study workflow.** The 8 steps were full-width rows; they are now a 4 × 2 grid of step cards (2 columns on tablets, 1 on phones). Each card keeps its number, title, description and link. The section is about 37 % shorter (1,412 → 883 px at 1400 px wide).

### About (`about.html`)
3. **A complete pathway.** The two-sentence callout becomes two cards: "Our work spans the entire research pipeline" as a numbered list of the 7 stages, and "We provide…" as 4 tiles (methods, reusable code, quality-assurance processes, analytical support) plus a highlighted note on local data control.
4. **Why collaborate.** The 4 reasons become a 2 × 2 grid of cards with headings. This also fixes invalid HTML: the `<li>` items sat directly inside a `<div>` with no list.
5. **Internal rules.** The 5 bold-led bullets become a grid of labelled cards (`<dl>`).

### Objective (`objective.html`)
6. **Assessing local needs.** The long sentence that was marked up as an `<h3>` heading is now a lead paragraph (it is not a heading). The readiness paragraph becomes a highlighted note starting "Readiness is study-specific."

### Partner (`databases.html`)
7. **Eight source-data domains.** The three availability categories become a three-part colour key that matches the flags in the table below. The closing sentence was merged into the intro sentence.

### Methods (`methods.html`)
8. **Structural mapping example.** The heading and intro stay visible; the 9-row mapping table moves into a "Show the mapping table" disclosure, closed by default. The OMOP/Sentinel filter buttons still work inside it (checked). The section is about 68 % shorter when closed.

### Site-wide
