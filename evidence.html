Case 01 · ASCVD

Transferable risk stratification, with visible implementation limits

The programme compared recurrent atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease risk and evaluated whether results remained consistent before and after common-data-model conversion.

What the case supports

  • Risk stratification was transferable across the represented East Asian settings.
  • The very-high-risk group remained clinically distinct.
  • Common-data-model results were broadly consistent overall.
  • Terminology mapping was the main source of distortion.

Treatment finding: treatment intensity does not match risk: 18% to 34% were prescribed high-intensity statins during hospitalization.

Interpretation rule: common models improve reproducibility, but they do not remove the need for fit-for-purpose validation in every database.

Case 02 · Atopic dermatitis

Three clinical outcomes across five data environments

The approved deck reports incidence rates and confidence intervals for AD-related inpatient or emergency-room events, complications, and treatment change. HIRA is not shown because the source slide did not include HIRA in this outcome figure.

Read the differences in context

These rates are events per person-year, not a database scorecard. Population selection, coding, clinical practice, coverage, and implementation can all contribute to the spread. The source deck reports that the common-data-model approach was feasible in an additional therapeutic area and could accommodate flexible disease algorithms.

Measure
Incidence rate with confidence interval
Unit
Events per person-year
