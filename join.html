Three pathways

Where could you contribute?

Data partner

Prepare a local research environment

Contribute source expertise, feasibility review, local implementation, quality validation, governance, and contextual interpretation.

  • Data owner or local PI
  • Analyst and data engineer
  • Governance or privacy lead
Researcher

Develop reproducible methods

Help translate questions into protocols, definitions, QA logic, analysis modules, and transportability assessments.

  • Clinical or methods researcher
  • Pharmacoepidemiologist
  • Statistician or informatician
Decision partner

Connect evidence to use

Clarify clinical, regulatory, policy, or post-marketing needs and the evidence standards required for responsible use.

  • Healthcare or policy organization
  • Regulatory or safety stakeholder
  • Industry research partner
