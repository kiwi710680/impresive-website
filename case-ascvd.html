Comparable risk stratification across East Asian data environments

Why this question

A clinical question and an implementation test

  1. The scientific question

    Does the guideline-based risk stratification distinguish recurrent risk across East Asian settings?

    The 2018 AHA/ACC/Multi-society Guideline distinguishes patients at very high risk of recurrent atherosclerotic cardiovascular events from those at high risk. The study examined whether the same stratification continued to distinguish recurrent risk across the represented East Asian settings.

  2. The implementation question

    Do CDM implementations produce interpretable results across data environments?

    The programme implemented the same ASCVD study question in common-data-model environments across countries, allowing results to be reviewed across sites and implementation pathways.

  3. Why test both

    One case, two complementary questions

    The case examined both whether the clinical risk stratification remained informative across settings and whether the shared study specification could be implemented consistently within different CDM-based data environments.

What came back

The risk gradient persisted, while treatment intensity remained limited

Reported HR range across CDM-based analyses

1.2 – 1.6

Across the represented CDM-based data environments, the very-high-risk group consistently showed higher recurrent ASCVD risk than the high-risk group, indicating that the shared stratification remained informative across settings.

High-intensity statin use during hospitalization

18% – 34%

Treatment intensity remained limited despite elevated recurrent risk. Between 18% and 34% of patients were prescribed high-intensity statins during hospitalization.

What the case supports

  • The stratification continued to distinguish recurrent risk in the represented East Asian settings.
  • The very-high-risk group remained clinically distinct.
  • Common-data-model results were broadly consistent overall.
  • Terminology mapping was the main source of distortion.

Treatment finding: treatment intensity does not match risk: 18% to 34% were prescribed high-intensity statins during hospitalization.

Interpretation rule: common models improve reproducibility, but they do not remove the need for fit-for-purpose validation in every database.
